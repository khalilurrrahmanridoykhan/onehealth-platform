IDSR Case-Definition Classifier

A real, explainable rule engine implementing WHO's Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response standard case definitions — 18 priority diseases ( case-definition checks, since malaria and dengue are each modeled as multiple definitions to match their real source structure), suspected / probable / confirmed, with the exact matched and missing criteria shown for every result.

Source: Kenya Ministry of Health, Division of Disease Surveillance and Response — IDSR Standard Case Definitions (a national adaptation of WHO AFRO's IDSR Technical Guidelines, 3rd edition, 2019).
