# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and constructively.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Critique ideas and evidence rather than people.
- Respect privacy, research ethics, and responsible health-data practices.
- Accept feedback and correct mistakes transparently.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information, and misuse of health data are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method listed in the GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed in good faith, kept as confidential as reasonably possible, and handled proportionately.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when this code is violated.
