# Ethics Statement

## Current repository data

This software repository uses publicly accessible, aggregate dengue surveillance counts published through the Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS), Bangladesh. The included demonstration data contain no names, contact details, patient identifiers, household identifiers, or individual-level health records.

Because the current work is software development and secondary analysis of public aggregate statistics, no human participants were recruited, no intervention was performed, and individual informed consent was not applicable. On that basis, formal institutional review board approval was not required for the current repository demonstration.

## Future operational use

This statement does not automatically cover future deployments or new datasets. Before using person-level, identifiable, restricted, linked, or non-public health data, implementers must obtain all required institutional, governmental, ethical, and data-governance approvals. They must also establish lawful access, data minimization, role-based authorization, retention rules, audit logging, and incident-response procedures.

The alert algorithm is not clinically validated and must not be used as the sole basis for patient care, outbreak declaration, or resource allocation. Operational thresholds and workflows require review by qualified public-health authorities.
